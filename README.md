# CivicLens – AI Triage for Citizen Complaints

**Turning citizen complaints into actionable municipal tasks.**

## About the Project

CivicLens is an AI-powered civic complaint management system that transforms unstructured citizen complaints into structured, prioritised and department-specific tickets.

It aims to help municipal authorities identify urgent civic issues, manage complaints efficiently and improve public service delivery.

## Problem Statement

Municipal bodies receive thousands of complaints related to potholes, overflowing garbage, broken streetlights, water leakage, drainage issues and fallen trees.

Many complaints are vague, duplicated or incorrectly routed, making it difficult to address urgent problems on time.

CivicLens aims to simplify this process through AI-powered complaint triage.

## Key Features

- Citizen complaint submission
- Multilingual complaint support
- Optional image uploads
- AI-powered issue classification
- Severity assessment: Low, Medium, High and Critical
- Automatic department routing
- Priority-based complaint dashboard
- Complaint tracking and management

## Issue Categories

- Roads
- Waste Management
- Water Supply
- Electricity
- Trees
- Drainage

## How It Works

1. A citizen submits a complaint through text or an image.
2. AI analyses the complaint.
3. The issue is classified into the appropriate category.
4. A severity level and reason are assigned.
5. The complaint is routed to the relevant department.
6. Municipal officers can view and manage tickets through the dashboard.
